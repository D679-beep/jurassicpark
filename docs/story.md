# The Lantern Crown — Story Draft (v0.1)

*Working title. All names are placeholders and easy to change.*

## Logline

A gentle emperor names his kind eldest son as heir so that his reign of mercy will outlive him. His second son, who has spent years bleeding on the frontier, believes mercy is getting people killed, and he takes the throne by force. Play the civil war from **either side**: as a knight defending the Crown Prince, or as a knight marching with the rebel Prince. Your knight rises through the three ranks of mana knighthood.

## Core Theme

**Is kindness a strength or a luxury?**

Neither faction is simply right. The Loyalists stand for compassion, law, and the future. The Rebels stand for survival, duty, and the present. Each campaign tests its own side's ideal: the Loyalists learn what mercy costs, and the Rebels learn what strength costs.

## The World

### The Empire of Solmara

Solmara is a temperate empire built over the **Wellspring**, a vast underground source of mana beneath the capital city of **Calderon**. Every emperor binds themselves to the Wellspring through the **Crown Vow**. That bond keeps the mana flowing evenly across the land. If the throne stays empty for long, the flow becomes unstable.

The northern border faces the **Hollow Wastes**. The creatures there, called the **Hollowed**, feed on mana. For generations the empire's best knights have held them back at a chain of fortresses called the **Ember Line**.

### Mana Knights: The Three Ranks

Very few people can form a **mana core**, a knot of living mana near the heart. Those who can, and who swear service, become mana knights.

| Rank | Title | What they can do | How rare |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Kindled** | Mana stays inside the body. Greater strength, speed, and endurance, and a blade sheathed in mana. | About 1 in 50 soldiers |
| **II** | **Radiant** | Mana leaves the body. Ranged strikes, barriers, healing, and one elemental affinity (flame, frost, storm, stone, or light). | A few hundred in the empire |
| **III** | **Ascendant** | Projects a **Domain**: a zone where the knight's will bends reality, such as slowing time or turning the ground to glass. Every Ascendant is bound by a personal **Vow**. If they break it, their core shatters. | Only **seven** are alive. They are called the Seven Pillars. |

**Ascension rule (central to the story):** A Radiant becomes Ascendant only by swearing a Vow they truly believe, at a moment that tests it. What a character swears shows who they are. Both player protagonists swear a Vow in Act III, and the player's earlier choices decide the wording.

### Key Institutions

- **The Dawn Lantern Order:** the imperial guard. Mostly loyal to the law of succession.
- **The Ashen Wolves:** the frontier legion that holds the Ember Line. Fiercely loyal to the Second Prince, who has commanded them for eight years.
- **The Mana Conclave:** the scholars and examiners who decide who may be trained as a knight. For centuries they trained only noble children.

## Principal Characters

### The Imperial Family

**Emperor Halden IV, "the Hearthfather"**
He is warm, patient, and beloved by common people. His **Hearth Edicts** opened the knight academies to commoners, cut taxes on farmers, and replaced forced conscription with paid service. To fund all of this, he reduced the garrisons on the Ember Line. That decision is the seed of the war.

**Crown Prince Elian (eldest, 26)**
Elian is kind, idealistic, and a Radiant of the light affinity, gifted at healing. He believes every life can be saved and every enemy reasoned with. He loves his brother and does not see the coup coming. His flaw is that his mercy is untested: he has never had to choose who dies.

**Second Prince Varek (24)**
Varek is brilliant, proud, and an Ascendant: the youngest of the Seven Pillars, with the storm affinity. He has spent eight years on the Ember Line watching soldiers die after his father cut the garrisons. He loves his father but thinks him blind. His jealousy of Elian is real, but it is not his whole motive. He believes Elian's reign will get the empire killed. His flaw is that he trusts only strength, so he cannot see when he is being used.

**Princess Mira (youngest, 19)**
Mira is a Radiant scholar who studies the Wellspring. She loves both brothers and refuses to pick a side at first. She appears in **both campaigns**, and the player's actions can win her over, or lose her.

### The Player Protagonists

**Loyalist: Aren Thale**
Aren is a farmer's son and among the first commoners admitted to a knight academy under the Hearth Edicts. He is a newly made Kindled, assigned to Elian's honor guard on the night of the investiture. He owes the Emperor everything, and the war tests whether that debt means loyalty to a person or to an idea.

**Rebel: Kaela Voss**
Kaela is a Kindled knight of the Ashen Wolves. Her home village on the Ember Line was overrun by the Hollowed the winter after its garrison was withdrawn. Varek personally pulled her from the ruins. She follows him because he was the only one who came, and the war tests whether he is still that man.

Aren and Kaela meet repeatedly over the course of the war as **rivals**. The same encounters play out from opposite sides depending on which campaign the player chose.

### The Hidden Hand

**Archmagister Sereth Vael, head of the Mana Conclave**
Sereth is outwardly neutral, courteous, and wise. In secret, the Hearth Edicts terrify him: if commoners can become knights, the Conclave loses its centuries-old power. He quietly feeds Varek's fears and supplies the rebels. On the night of the coup, Sereth's agent, not Varek, kills the Emperor. That turns an attempted arrest into patricide and makes peace between the brothers seem impossible. Sereth's goal is to let the brothers destroy each other, then rule as "regent" through control of the Wellspring.

### Other Ascendants (the Seven Pillars)

Recruiting or losing them is a major part of both campaigns. Draft list:

1. **Varek:** storm. Rebel leader.
2. **Lady Orsa Kell:** stone. Commander of the Dawn Lantern Order, and a Loyalist.
3. **Brother Tamsin:** light. An old priest and Elian's teacher. Leans Loyalist, but he is a pacifist.
4. **Grimm Holloway:** flame. Varek's second-in-command among the Ashen Wolves.
5. **The Widow of Frostmere:** frost. A neutral northern lord who can be recruited by either side.
6. **Iskander Rhue:** a mercenary Ascendant whose Domain warps time. He sells his loyalty, and either side can buy it.
7. **Archmagister Sereth Vael:** his affinity is secret, and he is hiding his Ascendant rank.

## Prologue: The Night of Ashen Lanterns (shared opening)

*Playable as a tutorial. The player controls both protagonists in alternating scenes, then chooses a faction.*

1. **The Festival of Lanterns.** Calderon celebrates Elian's investiture as Crown Prince. Aren, nervous on his first day in the honor guard, meets Elian, who treats him as an equal. Separately, Kaela arrives with Varek's escort from the frontier, carrying a report: the Hollowed are massing, and two more forts have fallen.
2. **The Petition.** Before the court, Varek begs his father to restore the Ember Line garrisons. The Emperor gently refuses and says the treasury must serve the living, not only the border. Elian takes his father's side. Varek leaves in silence. Sereth follows him.
3. **The Investiture.** At midnight, Elian kneels at the Wellspring altar to begin the Crown Vow.
4. **The Coup.** The Ashen Wolves seize the palace gates. Varek enters the throne hall to **arrest** his father and brother, not to kill them. In the chaos, a masked figure strikes down the Emperor. Varek reaches him seconds too late. Elian sees only his brother standing over their father's body.
   - *Aren's view:* he drags the wounded Elian through the servants' tunnels while Lady Orsa holds the hall.
   - *Kaela's view:* she chases the masked assassin and loses them, but finds a Conclave sigil-ring on the floor.
5. **The Choice.** The screen splits. The player chooses **Aren (Loyalist)** or **Kaela (Rebel)**.

The Emperor's last words are heard only in fragments. In the true ending, they are revealed in full: *"Both of you... are right. Together..."*

## Loyalist Campaign: *The Lantern Road*

**Arc:** Aren grows from a frightened guard into Elian's sword. Elian grows from a gentle idealist into a ruler who can be both kind *and* strong, or else he breaks.

### Act I — Flight (Rank: Kindled)
- Escape Calderon with Elian, Orsa, and a handful of guards. Elian is wounded and grieving, and he refuses to believe Varek is a murderer.
- Reach the southern city of **Lysmere**, whose people were helped by the Hearth Edicts. They take in the refugees.
- **Key choice:** Rebel scouts are captured. Executing them protects your position. Freeing them honors Elian's ideals but risks exposure.
- Aren's first duel with Kaela happens at the **Burning Bridge of Merrow Ford**.
- **Act end:** Elian swears to retake the capital. Aren breaks through to **Radiant**.

### Act II — Gathering Light (Rank: Radiant)
- Rally the southern provinces. Recruit allies: the Widow of Frostmere, the mercenary Iskander (who costs gold or honor), and Brother Tamsin (who refuses to fight unless convinced).
- Elian's kindness is tested repeatedly. A starving town begs for the army's food. A noble ally demands rebel prisoners be hanged. A peace envoy from Varek arrives. Is it a trap?
- Princess Mira reaches out secretly. She has found evidence that the Wellspring is destabilizing, and that someone is *siphoning* it.
- **Twist (late act):** Sereth visits the Loyalist camp as a "neutral mediator". Players who listen closely can notice his inconsistencies.
- **Act end:** The Hollowed break through the Ember Line, because Varek's forces are split between the war and the border. The Loyalists must choose: march on the capital while Varek is weak, or **send troops north to help the enemy hold the border**.

### Act III — The Siege of Calderon (Rank: Radiant → Ascendant)
- The assault on the capital. The final duel with Kaela happens on the palace walls.
- Aren faces Varek, or helps Elian face him, at the Wellspring altar.
- **Aren's Vow:** its wording depends on earlier choices. For example: *"I will never abandon the weak"* (high mercy) or *"I will see this war ended, whatever it costs"* (pragmatic).
- Ending branches below.

## Rebel Campaign: *The Iron Dawn*

**Arc:** Kaela begins as Varek's devoted soldier. She must decide whether she serves the man who saved her or the empire he claims to save. Varek begins certain and ends either redeemed or consumed.

### Act I — Iron Dawn (Rank: Kindled)
- Morning after the coup. The capital is under martial law. Varek is shattered by his father's death and publicly takes the blame to keep his army together.
- Kaela investigates the Conclave sigil-ring in secret. Varek orders her to drop it, because he cannot afford doubt now.
- Keep order in Calderon. **Key choice:** Riots break out. Do you crush them, or negotiate with the guild leaders?
- Aren's first duel with Kaela happens at the **Burning Bridge of Merrow Ford**, seen from the other side.
- **Act end:** Grimm Holloway, the flame Ascendant, recognizes Kaela's potential. She breaks through to **Radiant**.

### Act II — The Two Fronts (Rank: Radiant)
- Fight a two-front war: Loyalists in the south, the Hollowed in the north.
- Return to the Ember Line and the ruins of Kaela's village. This is the emotional heart of the campaign, and it shows why the Rebels fight.
- Varek grows harder. He considers burning Lysmere's harvest to starve the Loyalists, and he considers using Sereth's forbidden "Wellspring siphon" to empower his knights.
- **Key choice:** Kaela can openly oppose Varek, obey him, or secretly undermine him.
- Princess Mira is brought to the capital as a "guest". Kaela can protect her or guard her as a prisoner.
- **Act end:** The Hollowed break through. Word comes that **the Loyalists sent troops north to help**, or that they did not, depending on a hidden simulation or a mirror of the canonical choice. That decision shapes Varek's view of his brother.

### Act III — The Defense of Calderon (Rank: Radiant → Ascendant)
- The Loyalists besiege the capital. Kaela finally proves Sereth's guilt, or fails to.
- She fights Aren on the palace walls.
- **Kaela's Vow:** For example: *"I will stand between my people and the dark"* (honor) or *"I will follow my prince to the end"* (devotion).

## Endings

Each campaign has three endings, based on the player's moral track and key choices, plus one hidden **True Ending**.

### Loyalist Endings
| Ending | Condition (draft) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Throne of Mercy** | High mercy, Mira won over | Elian takes the throne and spares Varek, exiling him to command the Ember Line. The Edicts survive. A hopeful ending. |
| **The Iron Lantern** | Pragmatic choices | Elian wins but becomes harder. He executes Varek and rules through fear "for the people's good". A bittersweet ending. |
| **Ashes of the Crown** | Key allies lost | Elian dies in the siege. Aren holds the city, but the throne is empty and the Wellspring falters. A tragic ending. |

### Rebel Endings
| Ending | Condition (draft) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **The Storm Emperor** | Obeyed Varek, siphon used | Varek rules as an iron emperor. The border is safe, the Edicts are burned, and the empire is strong but cold. |
| **The Reformed Usurper** | Kaela opposed cruelty, Mira protected | Varek wins but, shaken, keeps parts of the Edicts. He spares Elian, who goes into exile. |
| **Kinslayer** | Siphon used, Sereth not exposed | Varek kills Elian. Sereth drains the Wellspring through Varek. Kaela turns on her prince in the final scene. |

### True Ending: *The Third Lantern*
**Unlock condition (draft):** Finish both campaigns, expose Sereth in both, and keep Mira alive and trusting both brothers.

A final shared chapter: Aren and Kaela, now Ascendants, bring their evidence together. The brothers learn the truth about their father's death and face Sereth together at the Wellspring. The Emperor's last words are heard in full. Elian rules, with Varek as Marshal of the North. Kindness and strength stand side by side, as their father hoped.

## Gameplay Hooks the Story Supports

These are proposals for later and are not locked in.

- **Rank progression:** The three knight ranks map onto the three acts. Each rank unlocks new abilities (inner strength, then projection, then Domain).
- **Moral tracks:** Loyalists track **Mercy ↔ Resolve**. Rebels track **Honor ↔ Iron**. These tracks decide Vow wording and endings.
- **Mirror battles:** Key battles such as Merrow Ford and the palace walls are playable from both sides. Some details only make sense once you have seen the other side.
- **Recruitable Ascendants:** Iskander, the Widow, and Tamsin can join either side depending on choices. Each one changes later battles.
- **Mira's trust:** A hidden value shared between campaigns, and the key to the true ending.

## Open Questions for You

1. **Genre:** visual novel with choices, turn-based tactics (Fire Emblem–style), action RPG, or text adventure? This affects how much of the draft becomes playable scenes and how much becomes cutscenes.
2. **Tone:** Should it stay closer to hopeful fantasy, or lean darker and more political?
3. **Emperor's fate:** Keep him dead (more drama) or have him fall into a coma (an extra stake for both sides)?
4. **Protagonists:** Keep Aren and Kaela as new characters, or let the player control the princes directly?
5. **Names:** Keep, change, or have a naming theme (for example, Latin, Persian, or Indian-inspired)?
