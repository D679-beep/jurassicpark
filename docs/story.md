# The Lantern Crown — Story Draft (v0.2)

*Working title. All names are placeholders and easy to change.*

### Changes from v0.1
- Crown Prince Elian is now an **Ascendant**.
- There are now **five Ascendants** instead of seven: four publicly known and one hidden. They are treated as **strategic weapons**.
- Ascendants must be **physically present** to affect a battle. All of them are in the capital on coup night because the investiture requires them to attend.
- The prologue is now a **playable, timed coup battle**. The rebels start with surprise and concentrated strength. Loyalist reinforcements arrive in waves. The rebels must **kill the Emperor, kill the Crown Prince, and imprison the Princess** before dawn.
- Varek now **orders** his father's death. The v0.1 twist where he only meant to arrest him is removed.
- The rest of each campaign branches on what happened during the coup night.
- Supporting characters who were Ascendants in v0.1 (Tamsin, the Widow, Iskander) are now lower-ranked or removed.

---

## Logline

A gentle emperor names his kind eldest son as heir so that his reign of mercy will outlive him. His second son believes mercy is getting people killed. On the one night when every Ascendant knight in the empire stands inside the capital, he strikes. He has until dawn to kill his father, kill his brother, and take his sister prisoner, before the empire's reinforcements bury him. Play the coup and the civil war that follows from **either side**.

## Core Theme

**Is kindness a strength or a luxury?**

Neither faction is simply right. The Loyalists stand for compassion, law, and the future. The Rebels stand for survival, duty, and the present. Each campaign tests its own side's ideal: the Loyalists learn what mercy costs, and the Rebels learn what strength costs.

## The World

### The Empire of Solmara

Solmara is a temperate empire built over the **Wellspring**, a vast underground source of mana beneath the capital city of **Calderon**. Every emperor binds themselves to the Wellspring through the **Crown Vow**. That bond keeps the mana flowing evenly across the land. If the throne stays empty for long, the flow becomes unstable.

The northern border faces the **Hollow Wastes**. The creatures there, called the **Hollowed**, feed on mana. For generations the empire's knights have held them back at a chain of fortresses called the **Ember Line**.

### Mana Knights: The Three Ranks

| Rank | Title | What they can do | How rare |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Kindled** | Mana stays inside the body. Greater strength, speed, and endurance, and a blade sheathed in mana. | About 1 in 50 soldiers |
| **II** | **Radiant** | Mana leaves the body. Ranged strikes, barriers, healing, and one elemental affinity (flame, frost, storm, stone, or light). | A few hundred in the empire |
| **III** | **Ascendant** | Projects a **Domain**: a zone where the knight's will bends reality. Every Ascendant is bound by a personal **Vow**. If they break it, their core shatters. | **Five** alive, four of them publicly known |

**Ascension rule:** A Radiant becomes Ascendant only by swearing a Vow they truly believe, at a moment that tests it. Both player protagonists can reach Ascendant in Act III. Because Ascendants are so few, a new one changes the balance of the whole war.

### Ascendants as Strategic Weapons

Ascendants work like a kingdom's siege engines and its last line of defense combined:

- **Decisive:** In a field battle, the side with an Ascendant present wins unless the other side also has one. Only an Ascendant can stop an Ascendant.
- **Present or useless:** An Ascendant affects only the battle they are physically at. Wherever one is committed, every other front is exposed.
- **Costly:** A full Domain drains an Ascendant for days. Overusing it risks cracking their core.
- **Political:** Each public Ascendant is a symbol. When one dies, armies lose their nerve. When one changes sides, provinces follow.

**Why all of them are in the capital at the start:** By ancient law, the investiture of a Crown Prince must be witnessed by every living Pillar (public Ascendant), who each lay their mana on the Crown Vow. That is the only reason Varek and his lieutenant left the Ember Line. It also leaves the border with **no Ascendant at all**, which matters later.

### The Five Ascendants

| Ascendant | Affinity | Side on coup night | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crown Prince Elian** | Light | Loyalist | His Domain, *Sanctuary*, heals allies and dulls enemy blades. He is the strongest healer alive, though no great killer. |
| **Lady Orsa Kell** | Stone | Loyalist | Commander of the Dawn Lantern Order. Her Domain, *Bulwark*, holds a gate or hall against an army. |
| **Second Prince Varek** | Storm | Rebel | The most destructive Ascendant alive. His Domain, *Tempest*, can break a battle line on its own. |
| **Grimm Holloway** | Flame | Rebel | Varek's second-in-command from the Ashen Wolves. His Domain, *Pyre*, burns everything in it, including the city. |
| **Archmagister Sereth Vael** | Hidden (void) | Secretly his own | Head of the Mana Conclave. Nobody knows he is an Ascendant. His Domain, *Silence*, suppresses other mana. That makes him the perfect hidden weapon. |

On paper, coup night is two Ascendants against two. The rebels win the opening because of **surprise**: Elian is locked into the Crown Vow rite, and Orsa is cut off from her knights. Sereth's hidden hand tips it further.

### Key Institutions

- **The Dawn Lantern Order:** the imperial guard, loyal to the law of succession. Most of its strength is in barracks *outside* the inner city.
- **The Ashen Wolves:** the frontier legion. Fiercely loyal to Varek. Two thousand of its veterans are inside Calderon as his "honor escort" for the investiture.
- **The Mana Conclave:** the scholars and examiners who decide who may be trained as a knight. For centuries they trained only noble children.

## Principal Characters

### The Imperial Family

**Emperor Halden IV, "the Hearthfather"**
He is warm, patient, and beloved by common people. His **Hearth Edicts** opened the knight academies to commoners, cut taxes on farmers, and replaced forced conscription with paid service. He paid for them by withdrawing garrisons from the Ember Line. Decades of sustaining the Wellspring have burned his own core down to almost nothing. He cannot fight.

**Crown Prince Elian (eldest, 26)**
Elian is kind, idealistic, and an Ascendant of light. His Vow: *"I will not take a life that can be saved."* That makes him nearly unbeatable as a protector and hesitant as an attacker. In a fight he holds back against his brother, and Varek knows it.

**Second Prince Varek (24)**
Varek is brilliant, proud, and an Ascendant of storm. His Vow: *"No one under my protection will be abandoned again."* He has spent eight years on the Ember Line watching soldiers die after the garrisons were cut. His jealousy of Elian is real, but he also believes Elian's reign will get the empire killed. He decides the only way to save Solmara is to remove his father and brother entirely. He knows it is unforgivable, and he does it anyway.

**Princess Mira (youngest, 19)**
Mira is a Radiant scholar of the Wellspring. She is the only person alive besides the Emperor who fully understands the Crown Vow rite. **That is why Varek wants her alive:** without her, a usurper who binds himself to the Wellspring risks being rejected by it. She is also the last legitimate heir if Elian dies, which makes her the Loyalists' rallying point.

### The Player Protagonists

**Loyalist: Aren Thale**
Aren is a farmer's son and among the first commoners admitted to a knight academy under the Hearth Edicts. He is a newly made Kindled, assigned to the Crown Prince's honor guard on investiture night.

**Rebel: Kaela Voss**
Kaela is a Kindled knight of the Ashen Wolves. Her home village on the Ember Line was overrun the winter after its garrison was withdrawn, and Varek personally pulled her from the ruins. On coup night she is given the hardest task of all: **capture Princess Mira alive**.

Aren and Kaela meet repeatedly as **rivals**, and the same encounters play out from opposite sides.

### The Hidden Hand

**Archmagister Sereth Vael**
The Hearth Edicts threaten the Conclave's centuries-old monopoly on training knights. Sereth gives Varek the plan, the timing, and the tool: a ward that **seals the Wellspring hall** and traps Elian mid-rite. Varek believes Sereth is an ally of convenience. Sereth's real aim is to let the brothers bleed each other dry, then take the Wellspring for himself while the throne is unstable.

### Supporting Cast (non-Ascendant)
- **Brother Tamsin:** an old Radiant priest, Elian's teacher, and a pacifist.
- **The Widow of Frostmere:** a northern lord with a strong household army. She can be recruited by either side.
- **Iskander Rhue:** a Radiant mercenary captain who sells his company to whoever pays.

## Prologue: The Night of Ashen Lanterns (playable, timed)

### Before the battle (shared, short)
1. **The Festival of Lanterns.** Calderon celebrates Elian's investiture. All four public Pillars arrive in the capital. Aren meets Elian. Kaela arrives with Varek's escort from the frontier and reports that the Hollowed are massing.
2. **The Petition.** Varek begs his father to restore the Ember Line garrisons. The Emperor gently refuses, and Elian takes his father's side. Varek leaves in silence, and Sereth follows him.
3. **Faction choice.** The player chooses **Aren (Loyalist)** or **Kaela (Rebel)** *before* midnight. The coup is played from that side.

### The battle: Midnight to Dawn

The night is measured by the **temple bells**. Every bell brings Loyalist reinforcements closer.

| Time | Rebel situation | Loyalist reinforcements |
|---|---|---|
| **Midnight** | Surprise. The lanterns go dark. The Ashen Wolves seize the inner gates. Sereth's ward seals the Wellspring hall with Elian inside, mid-rite. | Only the ceremonial palace guard (about 300) and Lady Orsa, who is cut off in the feast hall. |
| **First Bell** | Rebels still hold the advantage in numbers inside the inner city. | The **City Watch** arrives at the palace (about 1,000 ordinary soldiers, few of them Kindled). |
| **Second Bell** | Rebel pressure peaks. | The **Dawn Lantern barracks** break through the outer gates (about 800 knights, many Radiant). |
| **Dawn** | **Rebels are overwhelmed.** | The **Southern Legion**, camped outside the city for the festival, enters Calderon (about 5,000). |

**Rebel advantages:** surprise; 2,000 frontier veterans already inside the walls; two Ascendants free to act while Elian is sealed and Orsa is isolated; Sereth's ward.

**Loyalist advantages:** time; the reinforcement waves; Orsa's *Bulwark*, which can hold one location almost indefinitely; Elian, if he breaks the seal.

### Objectives

**Rebel (Kaela's campaign):** complete before dawn.
1. **Kill Emperor Halden.** Varek's personal objective. *Required.* If the Emperor survives until dawn, the coup fails and the mission must be retried.
2. **Kill Crown Prince Elian.** He must be reached before he breaks Sereth's seal. Once free, he is an Ascendant at full strength.
3. **Imprison Princess Mira.** Kaela's objective. Mira must be taken **alive**: if she dies, Varek loses his means of claiming the Wellspring.

Optional rebel actions that **delay the bells**: seize the bell tower, burn the canal bridges, keep Lady Orsa pinned with Grimm.

**Loyalist (Aren's campaign):** survive until dawn.
1. **Protect Elian.** Help him break the seal, then get him out, or hold until reinforcements arrive.
2. **Protect Mira.** Reach her before Kaela does.
3. **Protect the Emperor.** Possible only through a very difficult hidden route.

Optional loyalist actions that **hasten reinforcements**: ring the alarm early, open the river gate, free Orsa from the feast hall.

### Key moments
- **Grimm vs Orsa:** an Ascendant duel in the feast hall. Grimm's *Pyre* against Orsa's *Bulwark*. While this lasts, both are removed from the rest of the battle.
- **The Throne Hall:** Varek confronts his father. The Emperor does not resist. He speaks his last words to Varek privately, and the player does not hear them yet.
- **The Wellspring Hall:** Varek vs Elian. Elian's Vow keeps him from striking a killing blow. Varek has no such restraint.
- **The Princess's Tower:** Kaela vs Aren, their first meeting.

### Outcomes and what they change

The Emperor's death is fixed in every campaign that continues: the rebels must achieve it, and the loyalist player cannot normally prevent it. The other two objectives branch.

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| **Elian killed** | The Loyalists lose their leader and one of their two Ascendants. Lady Orsa takes command, and the Loyalist cause becomes **rescuing Mira**, the last heir. |
| **Elian escapes** (wounded) | The Loyalists keep a royal leader and an Ascendant. The war becomes **brother against brother**. |
| **Mira imprisoned** | Varek holds the key to the Wellspring. Rescuing her becomes a major Loyalist goal. |
| **Mira escapes** | Varek cannot safely bind himself to the Wellspring. It grows unstable sooner, and the war runs on a clock. |
| **Emperor saved** (Loyalist hidden route only) | Secret short ending: **"Dawn Unbroken."** The coup collapses. Varek flees north with the Ashen Wolves. The ending hints at a future war. |

**Canonical path** (the one used to write the main story and the true ending): **Emperor killed, Elian escapes badly wounded, Mira imprisoned.** The other combinations change scenes, leaders, and dialogue within the acts below rather than requiring entirely separate campaigns.

## Loyalist Campaign: *The Lantern Road*

**Arc:** Aren grows from a frightened guard into the sword of a broken cause. The Loyalists start the war weaker, with one or two Ascendants against the rebels' two plus a hidden third. Their whole campaign is a struggle to survive long enough to strike back.

### Act I — Flight (Rank: Kindled)
- Escape Calderon. With Elian alive, Aren carries the wounded prince out through the servants' tunnels. With Elian dead, Aren escapes with Orsa and Elian's body.
- Reach the southern city of **Lysmere**, whose people were helped by the Hearth Edicts and who take in the survivors.
- **Strategic problem:** The rebels can bring two Ascendants to any one battle. The Loyalists must avoid open battle and fight only where Orsa or Elian can be present.
- **Key choice:** Rebel scouts are captured. Execute them or free them.
- **Act end:** Aren breaks through to **Radiant** defending Lysmere's gate.

### Act II — Gathering Light (Rank: Radiant)
- Rally the southern provinces and recruit the Widow of Frostmere, Iskander's company, and Brother Tamsin.
- **Rescue Mira** from her tower in Calderon in an infiltration mission. Kaela is her guard. Success brings the last heir to the Loyalist side. Failure means she stays a prisoner until Act III.
- If Elian lives, his Vow is tested: a rebel commander who burned a village surrenders. His Vow says spare him. His soldiers demand death.
- Players who pay attention can find clues that someone else planned the coup.
- **Act end:** The Hollowed break through the undefended Ember Line. The Loyalists must choose: strike the capital while Varek turns north, or **send help north to defend the border alongside the enemy**.

### Act III — The Siege of Calderon (Rank: Radiant → Ascendant)
- The assault on the capital. Aren and Kaela have their final duel on the palace walls.
- At the Wellspring hall, Sereth reveals himself and his Domain, *Silence*.
- **Aren's Vow:** Its wording depends on earlier choices. For example: *"I will never abandon the weak"* (mercy) or *"I will end this war, whatever it costs"* (resolve). His ascension adds a new Ascendant to the war at its decisive moment.

## Rebel Campaign: *The Iron Dawn*

**Arc:** Kaela is Varek's devoted soldier who helped him commit an unforgivable act. She must decide whether she serves the man who saved her or the empire he claims to save.

### Act I — Iron Dawn (Rank: Kindled)
- Morning after the coup. The Southern Legion has driven the rebels out of the outer city, but Varek holds the palace and the inner city. Negotiations or siege follow, depending on how many objectives were completed.
- Varek tells the empire that his father was weak and the border needed a soldier on the throne. He takes full responsibility.
- **Kaela is assigned as Mira's jailer.** Their conversations are the moral heart of the campaign.
- **Strategic problem:** Varek and Grimm cannot be in two places at once. Holding the capital means abandoning the border, and the border has no Ascendant.
- **Act end:** Kaela breaks through to **Radiant**.

### Act II — The Two Fronts (Rank: Radiant)
- Fight a two-front war against the Loyalists in the south and the Hollowed in the north. Choose where to send Varek and where to send Grimm each turn.
- Return to the Ember Line and the ruins of Kaela's village.
- Sereth offers a "Wellspring siphon" that would give rank-ups to the rebel knights but drain the land. Varek is tempted.
- Varek pressures Mira to perform the Crown Vow rite for him. Kaela can help, refuse, or secretly help Mira escape.
- **Act end:** The Hollowed break through. News arrives of whether the Loyalists sent help north or struck the capital.

### Act III — The Defense of Calderon (Rank: Radiant → Ascendant)
- The Loyalists besiege the capital. Kaela uncovers Sereth's true plan, or fails to.
- Her final duel with Aren on the palace walls.
- **Kaela's Vow:** For example: *"I will stand between my people and the dark"* (honor) or *"I will follow my prince to the end"* (devotion).

## Endings

### Loyalist Endings
| Ending | Condition (draft) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Throne of Mercy** | High mercy, Mira rescued | The heir (Elian, or Mira if he died) is crowned. Varek is captured and imprisoned rather than executed. The Edicts survive, and Ember Line garrisons are restored. |
| **The Iron Lantern** | Resolve choices | The heir is crowned and Varek is executed publicly. The empire is restored, but harder and colder. |
| **Ashes of the Crown** | Key allies lost, Mira not rescued | The Loyalists take the city too late. Mira dies during the siege, the throne is empty, and the Wellspring begins to fail. |

### Rebel Endings
| Ending | Condition (draft) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **The Storm Emperor** | Obeyed Varek, siphon used | Varek rules as an iron emperor. The border is safe, the Edicts are burned, and the empire is strong but cold. |
| **The Reformed Usurper** | Kaela opposed cruelty, Mira cooperated willingly | Mira completes the rite for Varek on condition that the Edicts are kept. He rules, but she holds his leash. |
| **Kinslayer** | Siphon used, Sereth not exposed | Sereth takes the Wellspring through Varek. Kaela turns on her prince in the final scene. |

### True Ending: *The Third Lantern*
**Unlock condition (draft):** Finish both campaigns, expose Sereth in both, keep Mira alive with her trust in both protagonists, and follow the canonical path where Elian survives.

Aren and Kaela, now both Ascendants, combine their evidence. Facing Sereth at the Wellspring, the brothers finally fight on the same side. Afterward the Emperor's last words to Varek are revealed: *"I forgive you. Protect them, both of you."* Varek uses his Vow to seal Sereth into the Wellspring, sacrificing his own core in the process. He survives, but powerless, and surrenders to stand trial. Elian is crowned, with Mira beside him as keeper of the Wellspring. Kindness rules, but it has learned what it costs.

## Gameplay Hooks the Story Supports

These are proposals for later and are not locked in.

- **Timed opening battle:** The bell system sets the time limit, and optional objectives speed up or slow down reinforcements.
- **Ascendant deployment:** On the war map, each Ascendant is a single token that decides any battle it joins. Deploying one leaves every other front open.
- **Rank progression:** The three knight ranks map onto the three acts. Ascending in Act III adds a new strategic weapon to your side.
- **Moral tracks:** Loyalists track **Mercy ↔ Resolve**. Rebels track **Honor ↔ Iron**.
- **Mirror battles:** The coup night, the tower duel, and the palace walls are playable from both sides.
- **Mira's trust:** A hidden value shared between campaigns, and the key to the true ending.

## Open Questions

1. **Genre:** Visual novel with choices, turn-based tactics, action RPG, or text adventure? The timed coup and the Ascendant deployment both fit tactics or strategy especially well.
2. **Elian's death branch:** Keep it as drafted, where if he is killed he is truly dead? Or should he fall into the Wellspring and return later, changed?
3. **Ascendant count:** Is five right, or should it be even fewer, for example three, one per faction plus Sereth?
4. **Tone and names:** Hopeful or darker? Keep the current names?
